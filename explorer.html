Where the lesions are lost

Each bar splits the selected lesions by outcome at the chosen budget. Not seen: no read slice crosses the lesion. Rejected: the system reports it, but below the budget. Inconsistent: this seed never reports it, a sibling seed does. Not recognized: shown, and no seed reports it. Frontier MLLMs have one run, so their misses are split into not seen, rejected and not recognized only. Numbers on the right: recall at the budget, and M (mean recall at 0.25–2 FP per scan, all lesions).
